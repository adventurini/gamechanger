Background

Who I am & how I got here.

I started programming in high school and spent the better part of a decade building production systems nearly solo. The experience shaped how I think about architecture, ownership, and what it means to ship something that actually works.
High School → Early Career

Started with a text editor and a dial-up connection.

I started programming in high school — HTML, CSS, Dreamweaver. Built websites for local businesses before most people knew what a CMS was. No bootcamp. No computer science degree. Self-taught and problem-driven from the start.

For years I developed that way — taking on whatever was in front of me, building until it worked, iterating until it was good.

HTML/CSS Dreamweaver Self-taught
2019 — Riverguide

Head of Engineering at an Amazon agency. Built a production platform nearly from scratch.

I became Head of Engineering at Riverguide, an Amazon agency. With eventually one other engineer, I designed and built a platform from the ground up that ingested Amazon data daily and powered a team of 50+ agents with automations, manual configurations, dashboards, and reporting infrastructure.

This is where I mastered CTEs and advanced SQL, then became fluent across the full modern stack: Node.js, React, Next.js, GraphQL, and AWS infrastructure. I owned architecture decisions, deployment pipelines, and data modeling — all of it.

The platform handled real commercial scale, with real consequences when things broke. That environment sharpened my instincts around reliability, architecture, and ownership.

Node.js React / Next.js GraphQL AWS PostgreSQL / CTEs
2023 — The Hard Stop

Parent agency went bankrupt. They walked away from everything we built.

The parent agency went under. The software — years of work, built with care — was left behind. It was a hard moment, but also a clarifying one.

The decision.
Rather than immediately returning to engineering, I made a deliberate choice to build something — going deep into the youth sports ecosystem as a founder and operator, learning the space in a way no job would have allowed.
2023 – Present · Battle Juice

Built a brand from zero. 15,000 kids sampled before launch. $1M+ raised.

Starting in 2023, I built a youth sports brand from the ground up — sponsoring teams, showing up at tournaments, and building direct relationships with leagues, recreation authorities, and families across Metro Detroit. We launched in September 2025.

Everything the brand required, I taught myself — same as code. Since 2023 I've learned Photoshop, Illustrator, Shopify, and Claude-driven creative workflows, plus AI image and video generation to move fast without cutting corners. I created the characters, packaging, and visual identity with real elbow grease. I developed the formulations and the full branding system myself.

I built the website, rewards program, merch line, and all of the digital infrastructure: Next.js / Shopify storefront, sponsorship systems, affiliate structures, data models, and partnership tooling. No design agency. No turnkey vendor. Every tool, every page, every system.

Along the way we reached roughly 15,000 youth athletes directly before launch, signed anchor partnerships with local recreation authorities and soccer organizations, and closed over $1M in funding. The work gave me firsthand understanding of what families, coaches, and sponsors actually need from a platform like GameChanger.

May 2026 — Now

Stepping into the right role at the right company.

Our advisors and investors are replacing the day-to-day distribution grind with a network of beverage distributors. That frees me to work brand awareness on nights and weekends as the face of Battle Juice — and to bring my full engineering capability to a company I have wanted to build for or compete with for years.

I'm starting with Subscriptions Enablement. Once I've proven I can deliver there, I want a leadership path at GameChanger — not on day one, but as the next chapter after earning it.

I am not looking for a job. I am looking for the right place to build.
