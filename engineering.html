The Role + Beyond

Start with Subscriptions. Grow from there.

01 / PLATFORM
Brand account infrastructure
GameChanger already has team accounts, organization accounts, and fan accounts. A brand account tier — with its own data model, permission system, and content publishing pipeline — is a natural extension of existing infrastructure that opens up an entirely new category of platform participant.
02 / DATA
Sponsor analytics and attribution
If a brand sponsors a team and streams their games, every view is attributable. I would build the data layer that closes that loop — impressions from stream overlays, follow-through on in-app activations, and location-tied events — giving regional sponsors the measurement tools that national brands already expect.
03 / COMPLIANCE
COPPA-compliant NIL consent architecture
Youth NIL is fundamentally a consent and data architecture problem. The permissioned relationship graph — athlete profile, parental approval chain, brand match flow — needs to satisfy COPPA, protect minors, and still create genuine value. GameChanger already has the verified family relationships to do this right.
04 / SCALE
Supporting 10M+ games with room to grow
At 9% market share, the infrastructure needs to handle 10x growth without a rearchitecture. I design systems with that horizon in mind — for the scale that comes next, not just the scale that exists today.
