What I'd bring to the commercial team
01
Brand activation at the field level

GameChanger is a digital platform but the audience lives at physical events. Bridging those two worlds is a meaningful product opportunity. A sponsor who is already present at the field — running a booth, handing out samples, supporting the team — should be able to connect that moment to the families watching the game online.

That is a new class of content on the platform — one that deepens family engagement and creates a revenue line from thousands of sponsors who currently have no way to participate digitally.

New Revenue Channel Field Activation
02
Sponsor-as-videographer: the organic content play

GameChanger already allows coaches to designate videographers. A brand account could extend that naturally: a sponsor's rep shows up, records the game for the team they support, and gets credit for the content. The brand is present authentically. The family is served. GameChanger's content library grows.

Display that stream on a screen at the event. That is a brand activation that no other digital platform in youth sports is positioned to enable — and it starts from infrastructure GameChanger already has.

Content Strategy Sponsor Integration
03
The "For the Sport of Love" campaign was the right instinct. Here's the next level.

GameChanger's first national campaign celebrated coaches and parents — the unsung heroes of youth sports. That is the right emotional register. The natural evolution is creating ways for brands to participate in that story authentically, rather than interrupting it with generic ad units.

A sponsor who streams a team's games, supports the family at the field, and builds genuine community presence — that is not advertising. It is participation. GameChanger is uniquely positioned to be the platform where that happens.

The Brand Presence Flow

How a regional sponsor would operate on GameChanger.

Step 01 · Setup
Brand creates a sponsor account
Verified brand profile. Links to the teams they sponsor. Rep is designated as videographer for those teams. Profile is visible to families who follow those teams.
Step 02 · Game Day
Brand rep records and streams the game
A brand rep shows up and streams using their GameChanger account. The brand receives attribution for the content. Families watching experience the sponsor as a community participant, not an interrupter.
Step 03 · Activation
Brand posts the event activation
Post an in-app activation tied to the game location. A product sample station, a tent, a giveaway — contextual and visible to families already engaged with the game.
Step 04 · Boost
Brand amplifies to targeted audiences
Boost posts. Target by sport, age group, and metro area. Advertise sponsorship availability to coaches and league organizers. The most relevant audience is already on the platform.
Step 05 · Measure
Closed-loop attribution
GameChanger already has impression data, stream views, and team follower data. A brand analytics layer closes the loop — showing sponsors what their presence produced in actual engagement and reach.
Step 06 · Scale
Every local brand in America has this path
Thousands of regional brands sponsor youth sports teams. None of them have a clear path onto GameChanger today. This opens that door.
